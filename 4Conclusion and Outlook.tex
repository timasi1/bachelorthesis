\chapter{Conclusion and Outlook}
\label{chapter: conclusion}

In this work, we used a tight-binding model on a kagome lattice with different magnetic textures of noncollinear altermagnets. The model includes nearest neighbor hopping, Rashba-SOC and a coplanar magnetic exchange texture.\\
Further, we introduced symmetries of the magnetic order and of the complete system, applying them to the band structure, in-plane Fermi-surface spin texture and Berry curvature. Then, numerical computations were used to test the resulting symmetry constraints.\\

In the absence of SOC, we find the symmetry $\Theta_s[C_{2z}||E]$, constraining the energies and in-plane spins to be even in momentum, while the out-of-plane spin component and Berry curvature are odd.\\
Additionally, in the nonbreathing limit, the symmetry $[E||C_{2z}]$ was found, imposing evenness in momentum. Thus, in combination with $\Theta_s[C_{2z}||E]$, it forces the Berry curvature to vanish in the absence of SOC.\\
Symmetries in the presence of Rashba-SOC break if the real-space and spin-space transformations act inequivalent on the system.\\
Nevertheless, mirror planes $\mathcal{M}$ and time-reversal mirror planes $\mathcal{TM}$ in the $yz$ plane are found to hold even in the presence of SOC. Hence, they enforce constraints on the Fermi-surface spin texture and Berry curvature. These constraints are observed in our numerical results.\\
The T1 phase of \ce{Mn3Pt} contains $\mathcal{TM}$ planes, whereas the T2 phase contains $\mathcal{M}$ planes, which are related by a global spin rotation in the absence of SOC.\\
Similarly, the magnetic textures of \ce{Mn3Ge} and \ce{Mn3Sn} lead to time-reversal mirror and mirror symmetries, respectively, which are again related by a global spin rotation. \\
Due to the global spin rotation in the absence of SOC, we obtain results equivalent up to the global spin rotation.\\
Lastly, we found for \ce{Mn3Pt} the $[C_{3z}||C_{3z}]$ symmetry, which also holds in the presence of SOC and for \ce{Mn3Ge} and \ce{Mn3Sn} the $[C_{3z}||C_{3z}^{-1}]$, which is broken in the presence of SOC. Thus, \ce{Mn3Pt} exhibits no in-plane net magnetization, while \ce{Mn3Ge} and \ce{Mn3Sn} can exhibit one along the directions constrained by the $\mathcal{TM}$ and $\mathcal{M}$ planes in the presence of SOC, respectively.\\
Thus, we classify \ce{Mn3Pt} as an S-type altermagnet and \ce{Mn3Ge} and \ce{Mn3Sn} as altermagnets of M-type.\\
The calculated Berry curvature and spin textures support our introduced symmetry constraints.\\

In this thesis, we used a simple tight-binding model to develop a symmetry-based description. The model includes nearest-neighbor hopping and a precisely coplanar magnetic texture, while neglecting next-nearest- and further-neighbor hopping terms as well as intrinsic spin-orbit coupling.
Furthermore, we used parameters which were not obtained by a detailed analysis, thus the results need to be interpreted as a qualitative adaptation of the model to different magnetic textures.\\

In a subsequent work, the model could be extended by including intrinsic spin-orbit coupling, adding further-neighbor hopping terms or applying it to non-coplanar textures.
For the latter, an analysis regarding the $\mathcal{TM}$ and $\mathcal{M}$ planes would be interesting, similarly to the analysis of the in-plane $\mathcal{TM}$ symmetry studied in Ref.\cite{MnPtSOC}.\\
