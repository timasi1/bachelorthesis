
When a material permits momentum-dependent spin-split bands while having fully compensated magnetic order, we speak of altermagnets.
In this thesis, we investigate altermagnets under the influence of different magnetic textures, breathing distortions of the lattice, and Rashba spin-orbit coupling (Rashba-SOC).  \\

To this end, we introduce a two-dimensional tight-binding model for the magnetic order of the T1 and T2 phases of \ce{Mn3Pt}, and the magnetic orders of \ce{Mn3Ge} and \ce{Mn3Sn}. A unitary transformation of the tight-binding Hamiltonian is used to include the intracell positions in order to acquire the cell-periodic states. For the computation of the Berry curvature, we use the Fukui-Hatsugai-Suzuki method and additionally a composite representation for isolated composite band subspaces with included degeneracies.
Subsequently, the band structure, Fermi-surface spin texture, Berry curvature and Chern number are computed for the different magnetic textures.\\
Here, we derive symmetry constraints for the magnetic textures and compare them with the numerical results. \\

The T1 and T2 phases of \ce{Mn3Pt} give equivalent results in the absence of spin-orbit coupling (SOC) because their magnetic orders differ by a global spin rotation. Analogously, \ce{Mn3Ge} and \ce{Mn3Sn} delivers the same due their orders differ also by a global spin rotation.\\
In \ce{Mn3Ge} and \ce{Mn3Sn} we find a symmetry, imposing $C_{3z}$-symmetric behavior in the absence of SOC, where the spin-space is rotated inversely. In \ce{Mn3Pt} we find a combined $C_{3z}$ symmetry in spin- and real-space, now holding also for SOC.\\
The numerical results show behavior, consistent with the found $\mathcal{TM}$ planes, for the T1 phase of \ce{Mn3Pt} and \ce{Mn3Ge} and with the $\mathcal{M}$ planes, for the T2 phase of \ce{Mn3Pt} and \ce{Mn3Sn}.\\
In the nonbreathing limit without SOC, symmetries force the Berry curvature to vanish.\\
Breathing distortions break the symmetry that enforces evenness, while the remaining symmetry in the absence of SOC allows an odd Berry curvature in momentum, imposing a vanishing Chern number.\\
Using the derived symmetries constraints, we show that \ce{Mn3Pt} cannot exhibit an in-plane net magnetization, whereas \ce{Mn3Ge} and \ce{Mn3Sn} permits one along distinct directions in the presence of SOC. The examined materials remain spin-split in the absence of SOC, identifying them as strong altermagnets. Due to the permitted in-plane net magnetization, \ce{Mn3Ge} and \ce{Mn3Sn} are identified as M-type altermagnets and T1 and T2 \ce{Mn3Pt} as S-type altermagnets.\\
