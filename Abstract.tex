
When a material permits spin-split bands while having compensated magnetic order, we speak of altermagnets.
In this thesis, altermagnets are being analyzed under influence of different magnetic textures, breathing distrotiaons of the lattice and Rashba-spin-orbit coupling (Rashba-SOC).  \\
For this, we introduce a two-dimensional tight binding model for the magnetic order of the T1 or T2 phase of \ce{Mn3Pt}, of \ce{Mn3Ge} and \ce{Mn3Sn}. Using the tight-binding Hamiltonian, the band structure, Fermi-surface spin texture and Berry curvature are computed and discussed using symmetry analysis of the magnetic texture.\\
